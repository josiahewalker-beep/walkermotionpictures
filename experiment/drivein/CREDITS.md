# Drive-in world — credits and licences

## SET and CREATURES (src/set/)
- screen, booth, lamp_pole, popcorn_bucket, popcorn_kernel, moth, mouse, owl: original procedural geometry and textures
  (numpy + Blender), no third-party assets. Weathering helpers adapted from this site's own desert generator.
- "POPCORN" lettering on the bucket rasterised from DejaVu Sans Bold (Bitstream Vera / DejaVu licence, free to use and
  embed).
- Hero car (src/cars/saab9000*.py): the chrome "SOB" wordmark (invented make) is rasterised from DejaVu Sans Bold capitals
  (same licence) and extruded; all other car geometry is original procedural modelling traced from reference photos
  (photos used for measurement only, not shipped).
